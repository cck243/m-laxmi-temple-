# Jay Maa Laxmi Temple Donation Portal - Design Style Guide

## Design Philosophy

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep saffron (#FF6B35) - sacred Hindu color representing purity and spirituality
- **Secondary**: Rich gold (#D4AF37) - symbolizing prosperity and divine blessing
- **Accent**: Warm maroon (#800020) - traditional temple color for sanctity
- **Neutral**: Cream white (#FFF8DC) - representing purity and peace
- **Text**: Deep charcoal (#2C2C2C) - ensuring readability and elegance

### Typography
- **Display Font**: "Playfair Display" - elegant serif for headings, evoking traditional temple inscriptions
- **Body Font**: "Inter" - clean, modern sans-serif for excellent mobile readability
- **Accent Font**: "Noto Sans Devanagari" - for any Sanskrit or Hindi text elements

### Visual Language
- **Sacred Geometry**: Incorporating lotus patterns, mandala elements, and traditional temple architectural motifs
- **Warm Lighting**: Golden hour lighting effects reminiscent of temple aarti ceremonies
- **Traditional Textures**: Subtle marble patterns and carved stone textures as background elements
- **Divine Symbolism**: Lotus flowers, diyas (oil lamps), temple bells, and sacred geometric patterns

## Visual Effects & Styling

### Used Libraries
- **Anime.js**: For smooth counter animations and progress bar transitions
- **Typed.js**: For typewriter effect on temple descriptions and blessings
- **Splitting.js**: For elegant text reveal animations on headings
- **ECharts.js**: For donation progress visualization and transparency charts
- **Splide**: For image carousels showcasing temple architecture
- **p5.js**: For subtle particle effects resembling floating flower petals

### Animation & Effects
- **Hero Background**: Animated gradient flow in saffron and gold tones
- **Text Effects**: 
  - Typewriter animation for temple blessings and descriptions
  - Color cycling emphasis on important donation amounts
  - Split-by-letter stagger for section headings
- **Progress Indicators**: 
  - Animated donation progress bar with golden fill
  - Counter animations for total donations received
  - Smooth transitions for real-time updates
- **Interactive Elements**:
  - Hover effects with golden glow on buttons
  - Form fields with subtle lotus petal focus animations
  - Card lift effects with warm shadow transitions

### Header Effect
- **Background**: Flowing aurora gradient in temple colors (saffron to gold to maroon)
- **Content**: Layered text with temple name, divine blessings, and donation call-to-action
- **Animation**: Gentle pulsing glow effect on key elements

### Layout Principles
- **Mobile-First**: Optimized for smartphone donations with touch-friendly interfaces
- **Sacred Proportions**: Using golden ratio for layout spacing and element sizing
- **Temple Architecture**: Mimicking traditional temple layout with central focus areas
- **Accessibility**: High contrast ratios and clear visual hierarchy for all users

### Image Treatment
- **Hero Images**: Temple architecture during golden hour with warm, divine lighting
- **Background Patterns**: Subtle marble textures and carved stone details
- **Icons**: Custom-designed lotus, diya, and temple bell icons in the brand color palette
- **Photography**: Authentic temple interiors, donation counters, and festival decorations

### Interactive Components Styling
- **Donation Form**: Clean white background with golden accent borders and lotus-inspired field styling
- **Progress Bars**: Gradient fills from saffron to gold with temple bell milestone markers
- **Data Tables**: Alternating cream and white rows with maroon header backgrounds
- **Modal Dialogs**: Soft shadows with golden borders and traditional corner decorations
